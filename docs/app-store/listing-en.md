Keep your coding quotas in view. See remaining limits, reset times and local usage history together, with warnings before you run low.

Description:
Juicebar puts your coding subscriptions in the Mac menu bar. Colored lines show each selected limit, so you can check Codex, Claude and OpenCode together.

Open the popover for remaining quotas, reset times and an even-pace marker. Configure threshold alerts, pace warnings, forecasts and reminders for expiring reset offers when expiry dates are available.

Your usage history combines supported local logs in daily charts and an activity calendar. See the theoretical API equivalent of recorded tokens, with clear coverage for models that have no known price. These estimates are not subscription invoices.

Juicebar is open source under the MIT license. Buying this edition supports maintenance. A free direct-download edition is also available. Ordinary updates are included.

English and German. Requires macOS 14 or later and separately obtained provider accounts. Juicebar is an independent project, not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenCode.
