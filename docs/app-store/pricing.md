# Pricing proposal

Start at EUR 9.99, paid download with ordinary updates included. This is a proposal, not a configured or published price. Direct downloads remain free.

At EUR 9.99, roughly half as many purchases produce the gross revenue of EUR 4.99. This arithmetic does not predict demand. Taxes, platform commission and refunds affect proceeds.

Comparable published prices checked 25 September 2026:

- [Usagebar](https://usagebar.com/): USD 9 once.
- [TokenBar Pro](https://www.tokenbar.site/): USD 15 lifetime, one Mac.

These offers differ in scope and licensing, so they are reference points rather than evidence of an optimal price.

After a useful sample of store traffic and purchases, compare EUR 9.99 with EUR 14.99. Measure proceeds per product-page visitor, conversion, refunds and support burden in App Store Connect. Account for release publicity and traffic changes; do not interpret a handful of purchases as a result. No in-app analytics are required.

Check eligibility and enrollment in Apple's [Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) before launch. Its reduced commission is not automatic for every account. Do not promise lifetime maintenance or restrict compatibility fixes to new purchases.
